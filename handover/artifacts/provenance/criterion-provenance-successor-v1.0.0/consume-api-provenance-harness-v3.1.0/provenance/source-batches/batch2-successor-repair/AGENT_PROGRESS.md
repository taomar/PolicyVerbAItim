## Architecture Decisions

### Decision: Full Batch2 successor

- Context: one omitted canonical owner and one duplicate owner arise from a shifted Batch2 slice.
- Decision: emit a complete corrected Batch2 overlay and ledger for positions 39-76 rather than a position-only delta.
- Rationale: a full successor gives the merge owner one authoritative Batch2 input, preserves all unaffected records, and removes ambiguous overlay ordering.
- Alternatives: a two-operation delta was smaller in bytes but would require order-sensitive composition with the defective predecessor.
- Consequences: the merge session must replace, not combine, the original Batch2 overlay/ledger; the original Batch2 source attestation remains hash-pinned and unchanged.
- Validation: exact 113-ID partition, predecessor record hash equality, pairwise disjointness, combined guard preflight, mutation controls, and two byte-identical generations.

### Decision: Batch3 owns `HW-05:R4`

- Context: Batch2 and Batch3 both claimed canonical position 77.
- Decision: remove the Batch2 claim and retain Batch3 as the sole owner.
- Rationale: both records agree on criterion identity, canonical state, classification, non-scorability, no evidence upgrade, no replacement, and no human confirmation. Batch3 additionally binds the exact canonical pointer and record hash.
- Alternatives: Batch2 ownership was rejected because it would keep the shifted range and force Batch3 to change.
- Consequences: no evidence or classification is lost; only duplicate ownership is removed.
- Validation: material-conflict comparison passed for every integrity-relevant field.

### Decision: Fail-closed classification for `AIS-08:R5`

- Context: canonical position 39 had no owner.
- Decision: classify it as `structural_harness_not_product_law` and retain it unscorable.
- Rationale: its canonical record is question-inferred and unverified, has zero source/rule IDs and zero support, and the frozen boolean pointer cannot establish normative policy semantics. All existing relevant-question R5 records use the same structural boundary.
- Alternatives: mechanical support, semantic-pending support, unsupported policy, unavailable evidence, and product-decision classifications lack supporting evidence for this criterion.
- Consequences: ownership is repaired without changing scoring or claiming semantic confirmation.
- Validation: canonical criterion and evidence pointers/hash checks plus negative unsupported-upgrade and AI-as-human tests.
